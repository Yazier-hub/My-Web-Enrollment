Application Process

How to Apply

The Admission's online application process is quick, easy and secure. You can start filling in your application now, save your work, and complete it later. You will receive a unique applicant number, which allows you to access your personal application, change it and add documents and submit or start new applications any time you want.

Before You Start

There are some basic requirements you should keep in mind before you start filling in your application.

  • You will graduate from Junior High School this year or have already graduated.
  • You want to study and develop yourself in a new environment.
  • You are able to write and communicate in the language the studies are conducted in.
  • You will graduate ALS – Alternative Learning System, PEPT - Philippine Educational Placement Test
Learning Modality

Pure Online Modality

Who are ALLOWED to enroll?

  • 1. Those previously enrolled and willing to finish their studies but cannot attend the limited face-to-face modality due to their distance to the school and work schedules.
  • 2. Future enrollees considered as returnees or working abroad who had to drop out from school due to their present status.
  • 3. Those who are not fully vaccinated.
  • 4. Regular students with NO BACK SUBJECTS.
  • 5. IRREGULAR and TRANSFEREE STUDENTS are NOT allowed to enroll into the Pure Online Modality.

Pure Online Modality Guidelines

  • Students will need to access the Learning Management System (LMS) to read lessons, answer quizzes, do activities, and take examinations.
  • Students will have 3-hour monthly virtual consultation meetings with their subject teachers for each subject.
  • There should be at least 5 students enrolled per program per year level using the Pure Online Modality.
  • Students who will be SHIFTING from the Hybrid Modality to the Pure Online Modality within the semester need to get the approval from their respective program heads.

Once completed the registration, the Pure Online coordinator will contact the student to assist with the enrollment process.

Kindly visit the Social Media Office MV Campus and look for Mr. Johnnel Castro to get the Approval sheet.

Required Documents

Important Reminders

  • During your application process, please provide a valid personal email address. This email will be used by the Admissions and Registrar's Office to communicate with you. It is crucial that you regularly check your email for important updates, including instructions, acceptance notifications, the application link, and login credentials for your student accounts.
  • Our personnel are not allowed to correct any mistake in your application. After the application is accepted, it is forwarded directly to the proper authorities. Please make sure that all information presented in the application form are correct and error-free.
  • If there are still significant mistakes in the application, the Admission's Office will not accept the application. In this case the application is sent back to the applicant with directions so the applicant could correct the mistakes. If the mistakes are corrected, the application must be resubmitted by the applicant.
  • Your privacy is important to us. The information that you entered in your application form will remain strictly confidential and we will only use them to contact you regarding your application.
  • Your online readiness is also important to us. With the challenge that we are in right now, we would like to understand if you are ready for any of our learning modalities. Do not worry if you are not ready as of this time. We will give you enough time to prepare for your most convenient modality based on what we are offering.

Letter of Acceptance

After successfully completing the earlier parts of the enrolment process, you will receive an official e-mail of acceptance from us.

The letter of acceptance can be conditional or unconditional.
