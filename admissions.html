Learning Modality

Pure Online Modality

Who is allowed to enroll under the Pure Online Modality, and the guidelines that apply.

Who is allowed to enroll?

  • 1 Those previously enrolled and willing to finish their studies but cannot attend the limited face-to-face modality due to their distance to the school and work schedules.
  • 2 Future enrollees considered as returnees or working abroad who had to drop out from school due to their present status.
  • 3 Those who are not fully vaccinated.
  • 4 Regular students with NO BACK SUBJECTS.
  • ✕ IRREGULAR and TRANSFEREE STUDENTS are NOT allowed to enroll into the Pure Online Modality.

Modality Guidelines

  • Students will need to access the Learning Management System (LMS) to read lessons, answer quizzes, do activities, and take examinations.
  • Students will have 3-hour monthly virtual consultation meetings with their subject teachers for each subject.
  • There should be at least 5 students enrolled per program per year level using the Pure Online Modality.
  • Students who will be SHIFTING from the Hybrid Modality to the Pure Online Modality within the semester need to get the approval from their respective program heads.

Once completed the registration, the Pure Online coordinator will contact the student to assist with the enrollment process.

Documents

Required Documents

Prepare the original copy of the following documents for your application.

College New / Freshmen

  • Form 138 (Report Card)
  • Form 137
  • Certificate of Good Moral
  • PSA Authenticated Birth Certificate
  • Passport Size ID Picture (White Background, Formal Attire) – 2 pcs.
  • Barangay Clearance

College Transferee

  • Transcript of Records from Previous School
  • Honorable Dismissal
  • Certificate of Good Moral
  • PSA Authenticated Birth Certificate
  • Passport Size ID Picture (White Background, Formal Attire) – 2 pcs.
  • Barangay Clearance

Senior High School

  • Form 138 (Report Card)
  • Form 137
  • Certificate of Good Moral
  • 2"x2" ID Picture (White Background) – 2 pcs.
  • Photocopy of NCAE Result
  • ESC Certificate (if graduate of private Junior High School)
  • PSA Authenticated Birth Certificate
  • Barangay Clearance
  • Photocopy of Diploma
